## 📋 About This App

A comprehensive web application for drafting professional responses to:
- **GST Notices** (Sections 73, 74)
- **Income Tax Notices** (Sections 143(2), 148, 156)
- **TDS Notices** (Sections 200A, 201)

## ✨ Features

✅ **Upload & Extract** - Upload PDF/JPG/PNG scans → Auto-extract notice details
✅ **Figure Reconciliation** - Compare notice figures vs your book figures
✅ **Smart Reply Templates** - Pre-filled legal templates for each notice type
✅ **Quick Reply Mode** - Paste text directly, no upload needed
✅ **Case Law Links** - Quick access to Indian Kanoon, CBIC circulars, TaxGuru
✅ **Fully Editable** - Edit any generated reply before sending

## 🎯 How to Use (After Deployment)

1. **Tab 1: Read Notice** - Upload your notice PDF/image
2. **Tab 2: Compare Figures** - Enter your book figures
3. **Tab 3: Draft Reply** - Generate legal reply automatically
4. **Tab 4: Precedents** - Find relevant case law
5. **Tab Q: Quick Reply** - Paste text for instant reply

## 💻 Technology Stack

- **Backend**: Node.js + Express
- **OCR**: Tesseract.js + pdf-parse
- **Frontend**: HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JS
- **Fonts**: IBM Plex + Source Serif

## 📄 Notice Types Supported

- GST Section 73 (Short payment/ITC mismatch)
- GST Section 74 (Fraud/Wilful suppression)
- Income Tax Section 143(2) (Scrutiny)
- Income Tax Section 148 (Reassessment)
- Income Tax Section 156 (Demand)
- TDS Section 200A (Short deduction)
- TDS Section 201 (Assessee-in-default)
- Generic (Fallback for other notices)

## 🔐 Privacy

- All data processing happens on the server
- Files are NOT stored after processing
- No data sent to third parties
- Session-based only
